# Balamurugan Devadarshan

Founder of Clearway · Team DD Engineering · NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026

## Why I built Clearway

As a Singaporean born in India, I followed every step of the Chandrayaan missions, and I'll never forget the day Chandrayaan-3's Vikram lander touched down near the Moon's south pole — the first landing ever in that region. It made me wonder: how do mission teams decide where and when to land, so that a lander has sunlight for power and a clear line of sight to Earth?

At the same time, I've watched space change in front of us. Commercial companies are opening up the industry, and SpaceX's reusable rockets have made launches routine. Space is no longer only for a handful of agencies.

Clearway is my way of making it more accessible to everyone. It takes NASA's own data — laser-altimeter terrain, illumination maps and 5-metre site maps — and turns it into an answer anyone can understand: where to land, when, and how likely the lander is to survive.

## How Clearway was built

The idea, the choice of data, the design decisions, the testing against real NASA data and the Chandrayaan-3 analysis are mine. I used AI assistance (Claude, by Anthropic) to help write and debug the code. All data comes from the public NASA, ISRO and USGS sources listed under **Data sources**.
